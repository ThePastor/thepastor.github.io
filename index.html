Read Fast

Read at the speed of thought.

Drop in a book and read it one word at a time, centred on the screen, at whatever pace you choose. The words come to you, so your eyes don't have to travel.

  • Opens PDF, EPUB, DOCX, TXT, Markdown and ZIP
  • Words-per-minute control, pause, and a live page counter
  • Your library stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded

[ PLANNING ]

Syllabus Desk

Every deadline in your term, on one page.

Add each course outline once. Syllabus Desk pulls out the exams, quizzes and due dates, lets you check them, then gives you the whole term week by week.

  • Paste an outline or drop in the PDF or Word file
  • Every date lands in an editable table first, so nothing is guessed
  • Export to Google or Apple Calendar so reminders follow you

Approach

About

Built in Kamloops

JohnsonXCorp is an independent software studio in Kamloops, British Columbia. It started with a simple question: what small tool would make this easier? The answers became Read Fast, UNI Made EZ, Vamos and Syllabus Desk.

Every product is designed, built and maintained in-house, with the same care whether ten people use it or ten thousand.
