One-time setup

Taking payments

1

Create your Stripe account

Go to stripe.com and sign up with johnsonxcorp@outlook.com. Choose Canada, business type Individual / sole proprietor, and enter "JohnsonX Studio" as the name customers see. Stripe asks for your banking and ID details. Enter those yourself; nobody else should.

2

Make your standard payment links

In Stripe, go to Payment Links → New and create one link per package. For a deposit link, add a product such as "Starter Site deposit, $200" and leave "Let customers adjust quantity" off. For the Care Plan, choose Recurring · Monthly · $40.

  • Starter Site deposit (50%) · $200
  • Standard Site deposit (50%) · $350
  • Quick Fix · $150
  • Care Plan · $40 / month

For one-off amounts, use Invoices → Create invoice in Stripe. It emails the client a payment page and a receipt.

3

Track every payment here

On the Payments tab, click New payment, pick the client, paste the Stripe link, and click Copy link to text or email it. When Stripe shows it paid, click Mark paid. Interac e-Transfer and cash can be recorded the same way, with no link needed.

4

Card details never touch this site

Clients pay on Stripe's own secure page. This desk only stores the amount, the link and whether it's been paid, so there's nothing sensitive to leak.

Your saved payment links

Paste each Stripe link once. They're saved in this browser and offered when you create a payment.
