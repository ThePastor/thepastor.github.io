FAQ

Good questions

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. I build your homepage first so you can judge the real thing, not a sales pitch. If it isn't right for you, you walk away and owe nothing.

How long does a site take?

A Starter Site is usually live within a week of your deposit. A Standard Site takes one to two weeks, mostly depending on how quickly photos and text come together.

Who owns the website?

You do. Your domain is registered in your name, and once the final invoice is paid the site is yours to keep, with or without a care plan.

What if I already have a website?

I can fix what's broken with a Quick Fix, or rebuild it and move your domain over with no downtime.

Do I need the care plan?

No. It's there if you'd rather text me changes than learn to do them. Without it, I'll hand everything over and show you how hosting works.

Can you write the text and find photos?

Yes. I'll draft the copy from a short chat and use your own photos where possible. Real photos of your place and team always beat stock images.
