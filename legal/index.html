4 · Client work (JohnsonX Studio)

Every paid project is covered by a short written service agreement. It sets out the scope, price and timeline, and it takes priority over this summary.

What the client owns

  • Their domain name, registered in their name.
  • Their content: text, photos, logos and information they supply.
  • The finished website built for them, once the final invoice is paid in full.

What JohnsonX Studio keeps

  • Its templates, components, code libraries, tools and methods, which are reused across projects.
  • A permanent licence for the client to use those parts within their own site.
  • The right to show the finished site in its portfolio, unless the client asks it not to.

Payments

  • Free mockup. A mockup costs nothing and creates no obligation. Until a project is paid for, the mockup and its design remain the property of JohnsonX Studio.
  • Deposit. Work begins after a 50% deposit. If you cancel before work begins, the deposit is refunded in full. After work begins, it covers the work already done.
  • Final payment is due at launch. The site goes live on your domain once it's paid.
  • Care Plan. Billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time. Cancelling stops the next charge. Months already paid are not refunded.
  • Card payments are processed by Stripe. Card details go directly to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Taxes. Prices are in Canadian dollars. GST will be added if and when JohnsonXCorp is required to charge it.
